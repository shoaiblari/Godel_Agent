Hi everyone!


The Paper Video assignment is ready for everyone to get started! You can find
the assignment here: Paper Video Assignment
(https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs329z/assignments/paper_video.pdf)


In this assignment, you’ll record a 10–15 minute video presenting a recent AI
agent paper of your choice. You’ll explain the paper’s main ideas and results,
give your own critical take, and go beyond the paper by running the code,
walking through an example, trying a small experiment, or another extension!


A couple of notes:


The assignment is due Friday, November 13, at 11:59 p.m. PT. Submit an unlisted
YouTube link and your materials on Gradescope. The video is worth 10% of your
course grade, with another 5% for peer reviews. Peer review details will be
released separately.


Check that your paper is eligible before getting started. Papers listed as
required or additional readings for lectures through October 19, or discussed by
name in those lectures, are not eligible. Readings for later lectures are
fine. If you’re unsure about your choice, ask a TA!


Your critique and extension are a big part of the assignment. Together, they’re
worth 40 of the 70 points, so leave enough time to share your own insights and
show what you tried. A small experiment or a failed reproduction with a clear
explanation is fine!


Your slides, script, analysis, and narration must be your own. AI can help you
understand the paper, brainstorm, debug extension code, and get feedback, but
you may not use AI to make your slides. You should be able to answer questions
about anything in your video.


Good luck, and we’re excited to see what you explore!!!
